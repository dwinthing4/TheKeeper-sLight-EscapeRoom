The Keeper's Light

Cornwall, the night of the eighth of November, 1912

The keeper of the Pellow Point light, Elias Marrow, has not been seen for three days. You were sent by Trinity House to find out why the lamp has gone dark. The storm closed in behind you, and the door to the lantern gallery is bolted shut.

Through the window, the lights of a ship are moving toward the rocks.

Elias trusted no one. Whatever he left behind, he hid well.

The light is lit

The ship has turned for open water
